# Controller Enclosure (PCB build)

Enclosure for the rev 0.2 controller PCB and the MSP3526 display. A STEP model of the full assembly is attached to the GitHub release.

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| `Case.stl` | Main enclosure body |
| `Screen Panel.stl` | Front panel with display opening |
| `Rear Panel.stl` | Rear panel with connector openings |
| `PCB Plate Panel.stl` | Internal PCB mounting plate |
| `Dupont Connector Housing (Screen Side).stl` | Housing for the display harness connector |

PETG or ABS recommended for heat tolerance.

## Hardware

- 20 M3 × 5 × 4 mm heat-set inserts (Voron standard): 4 in the screen panel for the display, 4 in the PCB plate panel for the PCB, and 12 in the case (4 each for the screen panel, rear panel and PCB plate panel)
- 4 M3 × 6 mm standoffs between the PCB plate panel and the PCB
- 8 M3 × 4 mm (or 6 mm) socket head cap screws: 4 for the display, 4 for the PCB
- 12 M3 × 8 mm flat head screws for the case assembly
